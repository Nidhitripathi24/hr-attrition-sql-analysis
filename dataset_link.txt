Dataset Source

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance Dataset

Source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset

Description:
This project uses the publicly available IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset from Kaggle. The dataset contains employee demographic, job, salary, performance, and attrition information for 1,470 employees.
